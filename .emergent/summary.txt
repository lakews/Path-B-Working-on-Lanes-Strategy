**Key Technical Concepts**
-   **Async Parameter Injection:** Using a shared, thread-safe service () to pass guidance from slow analysis loops to a fast execution loop without blocking.
-   **Zero-Latency Telemetry:** Employing a  and a background thread to log high-frequency decision data without incurring disk I/O latency on the main trading thread.
-   **Markout Analysis:** A technique to measure adverse selection by comparing the trade execution price to the market price a short time after the trade.
-   **Hysteresis (Anti-Churn):** A control logic principle to prevent rapid state-switching by requiring a change to exceed a certain threshold before acting, used here to avoid unnecessary order cancellations.
-   **Semantic Rounding:** Explicitly rounding floating-point numbers to a set precision before comparison to avoid errors from infinitesimal floating-point artifacts.
-   **Cubic Inventory Skew:** A non-linear risk management technique that applies a small pricing penalty for small positions but a rapidly increasing (cubic) penalty for large positions to incentivize risk reduction.
-   **Adaptive Signal Smoothing:** An EMA-based filter with a jump detection mechanism that bypasses smoothing during sharp price moves (shocks) but filters out minor oscillations (noise).
-   **State Isolation:** Using dictionary-based memory () to ensure that historical data for one trading instrument does not leak into the calculations for another.
