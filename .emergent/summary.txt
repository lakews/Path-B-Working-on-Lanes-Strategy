**Key Technical Concepts**
-   **Four-Metric Drawdown System:** A sophisticated risk management system separating different aspects of drawdown:
    -   **Positional DD:** Performance of open trades vs. deployed capital.
    -   **Account DD:** Overall account health vs. starting capital (Primary Circuit Breaker).
    -   **Realized DD:** Performance of closed trades vs. peak realized profit (Secondary Circuit Breaker).
    -   **Total DD (HWM):** Industry-standard High-Water Mark tracking.
-   **Peak-on-Close Tracking:** A key architectural decision to only update  and  when a trade closes profitably. This prevents unrealized gains from creating phantom peaks and causing false circuit breaker triggers.
-   **Component-Based UI Refactoring:** Extensive modifications to the React frontend, involving adding, removing, redesigning, and repositioning components within  and .
