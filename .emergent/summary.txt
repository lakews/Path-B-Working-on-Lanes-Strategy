-   **The Bridge Pattern:** The  acts as a one-way bridge, allowing the fast Path B to consume signals from the slow Path A without blocking.
-   **Whale Direct Injection:** An optimization that bypasses the LLM for high-certainty signals (like large trades), saving significant latency.
-   **Universal Exit Engine:** A centralized  in the  ensures all open positions (HFT, Alpha, Sports, etc.) are evaluated against their specific exit criteria, preventing orphaned trades.
-   **Data Source Hierarchy:** A robust pattern where the system prioritizes the fastest data source (WebSocket, <1ms) and gracefully degrades to a slower fallback (REST API, ~91ms) if the primary source fails.
-   **Equity vs. Capital Accounting:** Differentiating between  (the true value of the portfolio) and  (cash on hand) for accurate performance tracking.
